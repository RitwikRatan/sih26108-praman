The Challenge

Procurement officers frequently encounter complex technical specifications across diverse domains. Identifying applicable Indian Standards (IS), verifying active versions, and checking specification completeness manually is time-consuming.

Our Solution

PRAMAN processes raw text requirements or uploaded tender PDFs, extracts key parameters, matches them against the Indian Standards catalog using explainable logic, identifies allied standards, and generates procurement readiness reports.
